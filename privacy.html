Jarvis — Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

What Jarvis is

Jarvis is a personal assistant application operated by, and used only by, its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the owner.

What data it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google sign-in, Jarvis accesses the owner's own Gmail messages and Google Calendar events.

How the data is used

To read and summarise the owner's mail, to prepare draft replies that the owner reviews before anything is sent, and to display the owner's upcoming calendar events. Jarvis does not send email on its own.

Where the data is stored

Google data is processed on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored locally on that computer. No Google user data is uploaded to, stored on, or transmitted through any server operated by Jarvis or by third parties, other than Google's own services.

Sharing

No Google user data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone. Jarvis does not use Google user data for advertising.

Limited use

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access removes Jarvis's ability to read the data; locally stored tokens then stop working.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen for this application.
